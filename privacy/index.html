Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Humancha processes information when you use its website and CAPTCHA API.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Humancha, the service may process:

  • CAPTCHA session identifiers.
  • Hashed CAPTCHA answers used for verification.
  • CAPTCHA expiration timestamps.
  • Verification attempts and results.
  • IP-derived identifiers used for rate limiting.
  • Technical request information processed by hosting infrastructure.

You do not need to provide your name or email address to complete a CAPTCHA challenge.

2. CAPTCHA Sessions

Humancha creates temporary CAPTCHA sessions to manage challenges and verification.

CAPTCHA challenges are configured to expire after a maximum of 120 seconds.

The database may retain associated records until the scheduled cleanup process removes expired sessions. Challenge expiration does not necessarily mean every associated record or server log is immediately deleted.

3. Answer Protection

Humancha stores a cryptographic hash of the expected CAPTCHA answer rather than the original answer in its session records.

This hash is used to compare submitted answers during verification.

4. IP Addresses and Rate Limiting

Humancha processes IP-derived information to help enforce request limits and protect the API against abuse.

The application derives a hashed identifier for rate-limit tracking.

Hosting providers and other infrastructure services may independently process IP addresses and technical request information in their logs.

5. Third-Party Services

Humancha uses third-party infrastructure, including:

  • Vercel: Website and API hosting.
  • Supabase: Database storage and verification operations.
  • cron-job.org: Scheduled maintenance and expired-session cleanup.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

Humancha's CAPTCHA functionality may use browser-side resources to display and operate challenges.

We do not intentionally use CAPTCHA cookies for advertising or behavioral tracking.

7. Data Security

Humancha uses measures such as cryptographic hashing, signed challenge tokens, request limits, and restricted database access to help protect its service and associated information.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

CAPTCHA session records are temporary and are subject to expiration and scheduled cleanup.

Other information, including infrastructure logs, may be retained according to the policies and retention settings of the relevant service providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Humancha changes. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
